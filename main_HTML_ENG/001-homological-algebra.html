The only difference from a chain complex is that the differential raises the degree instead of lowering it. Note

The reason the distinction deserves its own notation is practical: applying a contravariant functor such as to a chain complex reverses the arrows and produces a cochain complex. This is exactly why Ext is computed by cohomology while Tor is computed by homology.
